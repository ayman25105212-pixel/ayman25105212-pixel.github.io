Fit Quest: Block Puzzle - Privacy Policy

A2M Studio. Last updated 16 September 2026.

This page is the privacy policy for Fit Quest: Block Puzzle (com.a2mstudio.fitquest), published by A2M Studio. It describes this game and no other. It says what the game collects, why, who receives it and how long it is kept.

There is no account. The game never asks for your name, your email address, your phone number or any payment details, and there is nothing to sign in to. Nothing the game sends is attached to an account, because no account exists.

Who this game is for

Fit Quest: Block Puzzle is intended for players aged 18 and over, which is the target age declared on its Google Play listing. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Advertising

The game shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. There are two kinds:

  • a rewarded video that you choose. The game says what the advertisement gives before it plays, and nothing happens if you decline;
  • a full screen advertisement between levels, which you do not choose. It is never shown during a move and never after a loss, and the limits on how often it can appear are printed on the game's Settings screen.

To select and measure advertisements, Google collects device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, together with your IP address, the approximate location Google derives from it, and how you interacted with the advertisement. That information is collected and it is shared with Google, which uses it as an independent advertising provider. A2M Studio receives only aggregate counts and earnings. We never receive, store or have access to your advertising ID, or to any profile built from it.

Google's own description of what it does with this data is at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Consent, and how to change your mind

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised advertisement is requested. It asks through Google's User Messaging Platform, using the consent message A2M Studio has published for those regions, and it requests no advertising until you have answered.

You can change or withdraw that answer at any time, from Settings > Ad privacy options inside the game. That entry appears whenever Google tells the game that privacy options are available to you.

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time from the phone's own settings, under Privacy and then Ads.

Play statistics

The game sends a small stream of anonymous play statistics, so that the next version can be better: which levels are too hard, where people stop, and whether an advertisement was shown or refused.

What is sent. Every line carries:

  • an install identifier, a random value the game generates on your device the first time it runs. It is attached to nothing: not to an account, not to your advertising ID, not to any hardware serial number, and it is gone when you remove the game;
  • a random session identifier, and the time in UTC;
  • the event, which is one of: the game becoming ready; a session starting or ending; a level or a daily challenge started, finished or lost; a booster used; a daily gift taken; an advertisement offered, shown, completed, closed or refused; and a change in the coin balance;
  • the numbers that belong to that event, such as the level number, the attempt number and how long it took;
  • the app version and the platform, and once per session the device model, the operating system version, the memory size and the screen size.

What is never sent. Nothing you type. No name, no email address, no phone number, no contacts, no photographs, no files, no location read from the device, no advertising ID, and nothing at all about any other app on your phone.

Who receives it. Only A2M Studio. The rows are held for us by Supabase Inc., our database provider, and they are not shared with anyone else. In Google Play's Data safety vocabulary this is App activity (App interactions) and App info and performance (Diagnostics): collected, not shared, for Analytics.

How long it is kept. These rows are automatically deleted within 90 days.

What the game writes on your device

Your saved game (your progress, your settings, your coins and boosters, your stars and your daily streak) is written in the game's own storage on your device and read back from there. We do not upload that file and we cannot read it. Removing the game deletes it. That is separate from the advertising and the play statistics described above, which do leave your device.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases in this game. No screen in it asks you for money. Everything is earned by playing, or by watching an advertisement you chose to watch.

Security, and where the data goes

Everything the game sends travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Our providers, Google and Supabase Inc., may process it on servers outside your country.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw your advertising consent at any time from Settings > Ad privacy options in the game.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID from Android's own settings.
  • Remove the game. That deletes everything it has written on your device and ends all collection; the play statistics already sent carry only the random install identifier, and they are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this same address, with a new date at the top.
